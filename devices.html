Device compatibility

Which devices run Transcry

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Transcry has three ways to transcribe. Live (your device’s own speech recognizer) and Online (using your own cloud API key) work on virtually any modern Android. On-device transcription — fully private and offline — is the most demanding: it runs a speech model directly on your device, so it needs a reasonably capable processor and enough memory. On some entry-level devices it can’t run — and when that happens the app tells you and offers one tap to Live.

The three modes

  • On-device (private, offline) — runs the Whisper speech model locally via WebAssembly. Needs a reasonably modern 64-bit chip, enough RAM for the model you pick (the larger models want ~4 GB or more), and an up-to-date Android System WebView. You choose the model size in Settings; smaller models are lighter.
  • Live (device recognizer) — uses Android’s own built-in speech recognition. Works on nearly all devices with Google services. No model download.
  • Online (your API key) — sends the audio you choose to the provider you set up (Groq, OpenAI or AssemblyAI) with your own key. Works on any device with internet.

If on-device doesn’t work on your device

You won’t be stuck. The app detects the failure and shows a clear message with a one-tap “Switch to Live mode” button; you can also add an online key in Settings → Online transcription. Transcription still works — just through Live or Online instead.

General guidance

  • On-device works best on recent mid-range and flagship devices — roughly 4 GB RAM or more, a current 64-bit chip, and an up-to-date Android System WebView.
  • On a lower-end device, pick a smaller model (Settings) first; if on-device still won’t run, use Live or Online.
  • Keep Android System WebView updated from the Play Store — the on-device engine relies on it.

Help us grow this list

If on-device transcription fails on your device, please tell us the device so we can add a confirmed result here:

  • Tap Send report on the “Transcry closed unexpectedly” banner if it appears, or
  • use Settings → Feedback and include your exact device model.

The three modes are independent: even where on-device isn’t available, Live (fully on-device, via Android’s recognizer) and Online keep working, so Transcry still transcribes on virtually every device.
