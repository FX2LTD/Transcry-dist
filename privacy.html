Transcry — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Transcry (“the app”) is a voice-to-text app published by FX2 (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: Transcry is built to keep your audio and text on your device. We do not run analytics, we do not have user accounts, and we never sell your data.

Who the developer is

FX2 — contact: info@fx2.it

What the app does with your voice and text

How your audio is handled depends on which transcription mode you choose in the app:

  • On-device transcription (default, fully offline). When you use the downloaded speech model, your audio is transcribed entirely on your device. The audio and the resulting text are not sent anywhere.
  • Live mode (device speech recognition). Live mode uses your device’s own speech-recognition service (for example, the one provided by Google on your phone). Your spoken audio is processed by that on-device/system service under its own privacy terms — Transcry does not receive or store a copy beyond the transcript it shows you.
  • Online transcription with your own API key (BYOK). If you enter your own API key for a cloud provider, Transcry sends the audio you choose to transcribe directly to the provider you selected, using your key:
    • Groq, OpenAI, or AssemblyAI (your choice).
    • For AssemblyAI speaker labels, the whole original audio file is uploaded to AssemblyAI for processing.
    • The audio goes straight from your device to that provider — it does not pass through FX2’s servers. Your use of a provider is governed by that provider’s privacy policy and your account with them.
  • Translation (optional). If you translate a transcript using your own cloud key, the text to translate is sent directly to the provider you configured (Groq or OpenAI). Offline translation, when available, stays on your device.

You choose the mode. Nothing is uploaded unless you pick an online option and provide your own key.

What is stored on your device

  • Transcripts and translations you save, and optionally the original audio of saved items, are stored only on your device (in the app’s local storage). They are not uploaded by this feature. Deleting an item, or the app, removes them.
  • Your settings, including any API keys you enter, are stored only on your device so the app can reuse them. Keys are not transmitted to FX2.

The speech model download

The on-device speech model is downloaded from huggingface.co the first time you choose to install it. This is a normal file download of an ML model; no personal data is sent in order to fetch it.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

If you buy a plan (one-time unlock or a subscription), the purchase is handled by Google Play Billing. To confirm what you bought, the app sends the Google purchase token and an anonymous, randomly-generated install identifier to our verification server, which checks it with Google and records your plan status. This server never receives your audio, transcripts, or API keys, and the install identifier is not linked to your name, email, or Google account. Google processes the payment under Google’s own privacy policy.

Feedback

If you use the in-app feedback option, the app opens your own email app with a message addressed to info@fx2.it. Nothing is sent automatically — you decide whether to send it, and you can see and edit everything (including any diagnostic details) before you do. If you email us, we receive what you send.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or tracking. The app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • No accounts. There is no sign-up and no profile.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data with third parties for their own purposes. (The only third parties involved are the ones you choose: your device’s speech service, any cloud provider you supply a key for, Google for payments, and Hugging Face for the model download.)

Children

Transcry is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed at children.

Your choices

  • Use on-device mode to keep everything offline.
  • Don’t enter a cloud API key if you don’t want any audio or text to leave your device for a third-party provider.
  • Delete saved transcripts/audio in the app at any time, or uninstall the app to remove all locally-stored data.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The “Last updated” date above reflects the current version.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: info@fx2.it
